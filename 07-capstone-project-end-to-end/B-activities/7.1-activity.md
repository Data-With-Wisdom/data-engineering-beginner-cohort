## Activity 7.1: Capstone Planning Session

### Goal
Help students turn a broad project idea into a clear plan.

### Instructions
1. Choose a project theme such as sales, customers, or employee data.
2. Identify the main business question the project should answer.
3. List the data source, the cleaning steps, and the final output.
4. Decide which tools from the course you will use.
5. Write a short plan in 5 to 7 bullet points.

### Reflection questions
- What is the most important thing your project should show?
- What part of the workflow feels easiest?
- What part will need the most care?
