# Lesson 6.5: Spark vs Pandas Decision Framework

## Why this matters
Pandas is great for small and medium-sized datasets, but not every workload should use it. When data grows too large or too fast, teams may need a different tool.

## Pandas
Pandas is easy to learn and works well for:
- CSV and JSON files
- local analysis
- small-to-medium datasets
- quick transformations

## Spark
Spark is designed for larger-scale processing across many machines. It is useful when:
- data is too large for one computer
- processing needs to be distributed
- pipelines must be faster and more scalable

## Decision framework
Ask these questions:
1. Is the data small enough for one machine?
2. Do I need fast distributed processing?
3. Is the workload batch or streaming?
4. Does the team need scalability for future growth?

## Key takeaway
Pandas is often the best tool for learning and local work. Spark becomes relevant when the workload grows beyond simple single-machine processing.
